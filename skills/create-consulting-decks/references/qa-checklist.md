# QA Checklist

Use this before final handoff.

## Storyline

- The deck answers a concrete decision or objective.
- The first slide or executive summary states the recommendation when available.
- Each slide has one governing message.
- Slide order builds a clear argument, not a collection of analyses.
- The conclusion follows from the evidence shown.

## Slide Quality

- Titles are takeaway statements, not topic labels.
- Supporting points are parallel and concise.
- Every chart/table has units, time period, and clear labels.
- The most important number or implication is visually emphasized.
- Dense backup material is in the appendix.

## Evidence

- Important claims have a source, assumption, or clear rationale.
- Unknowns are labeled as assumptions, estimates, or hypotheses.
- Numbers are internally consistent across slides.
- Risk language avoids overclaiming and avoids guarantees.

## Visual Consistency

- Margins, footer, page number, typography, and colors are consistent.
- Text is readable at presentation distance.
- No text overlaps, clips, or spills outside slide bounds.
- Chart colors and legends are consistent.
- Image-generated slides are single 16:9 slides, not contact sheets or mockups.

## Brand And Legal Safety

- Do not use consulting firm logos or copied templates.
- Do not claim the deck is produced by BCG, McKinsey, Bain, Big 4, or another firm unless the user owns that brand context.
- Use "consulting-grade" or "strategy consulting style" rather than pretending to be a specific firm.

## Output

- Manifest validates without errors.
- PPTX exists in `outputs/`.
- Slide images, if any, are stored in `assets/images/<project-name>/`.
- Outline or notes exist in `notes/`.
- Final response mentions any missing data, assumptions, or slides that still need image inspection.
